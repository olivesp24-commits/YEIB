# YEIB Investment Fund — Design System v1.1

**Built for founders who are proving the case.**

Color, type, voice, and components for YEIB's brand and website.

## 01 — Color

### A confident evergreen system, one bright signal.

| Swatch | Name | Role | Hex |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟢 (dark) | Evergreen | Primary — foundation | `#003124` |
| 🟢 (bright) | Mint Leaf | Primary — action | `#00BE93` |
| ⬜ (pale) | Mint Cream | Base | `#F2FBF6` |
| 🟠 | Tiger Orange | Accent — rare use | `#F88404` |
| 🟤 (tan) | Pale Oak | Supporting surface | `#E1C9B3` |

### 01b — Colour Discipline

**Every field has one job. No colour does two.**

**Dark Fields**
Evergreen carries Mint Leaf for figures and role lines, Pale Oak for rules and labels, and Mint Cream for body type.

**Light Fields**
Mint Cream and white take Evergreen as the primary and the only ink. Mint Leaf and Tiger Orange appear here as fields, marks, and fills, never as type.

**Tiger Orange — Campaign & Callouts**
Deadlines, application windows, event calls to action (CTA), and the campaign band. Tiger Orange is a field rather than an ink, and it takes Evergreen type and, in immaterial contexts, white. Never for core institutional messaging.

**Contrast ratios**

| Combination | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Mint Cream on Evergreen | 13.59 |
| Pale Oak on Evergreen | 9.02 |
| Mint Leaf on Evergreen | 6.00 |
| Evergreen on Tiger Orange | 5.86 |
| Evergreen on Mint Cream | 13.59 |
| White on Tiger Orange | 2.53 |
| Mint Leaf on Mint Cream | 2.27 |
